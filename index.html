Philippines

The Philippines is a Southeast Asian country made up of over 7,000 islands located in the western Pacific Ocean. Known for its rich cultural heritage, the nation blends Malay, Spanish, American, and indigenous influences. Its capital is Manila, and its largest city is Quezon City. The Philippines is famous for its stunning beaches, lush mountains, and vibrant festivals. Filipino hospitality, strong family ties, and love for music and food are central to its identity. The country’s economy is diverse, driven by services, remittances, and a growing technology sector.
